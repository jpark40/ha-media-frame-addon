Video compatibility

Recommended settings for dependable silent playback in Android WebView.

Fully Kiosk

  • Enable Autoplay: on
  • Enable JavaScript: on
  • Enable Fullscreen Videos: off
  • Android System WebView: current version

Safest video file

  • MP4 container with H.264 AVC video
  • 8-bit YUV 4:2:0, 1080p or lower, 30 fps or lower
  • Fast-start metadata; no audio track is preferred
  • Avoid HEVC/H.265, 10-bit HDR, Dolby Vision and unusual MOV files
